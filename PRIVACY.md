# Privacy Statement

Job Application Autofill is designed to keep your personal job application details under your control.

## Where Your Information Is Stored

The profile details you enter in the popup are stored by Chrome in your own browser profile using local Chrome extension storage through `chrome.storage.local`.

This can include details such as your name, email, phone number, address, LinkedIn URL, portfolio URL, custom answers, and resume-derived profile sections.

If you upload a resume, the extracted resume text and parsed profile sections, such as education, experience, skills, and languages, are also stored in your own Chrome extension storage until you delete the resume from the extension.

This information is not stored in this repository, not stored on a server controlled by the developer, and not visible to the developer.

## Developer Access

The developer of this extension does not receive, collect, view, or have access to the information you save in the extension.

Each user's saved data stays in that user's own Chrome browser profile on that device. The extension does not sync this data through the developer or through a developer-controlled service.

## What This Extension Does Not Do

This extension does not:

- Sell your data.
- Use analytics or tracking scripts.
- Send your profile details to an external server.
- Upload your resume file to an external server.
- Share your saved profile with the developer.
- Automatically submit job applications.
- Fill fields unless you click the **Fill Page** button.

## Chrome Local Storage

Because the extension uses `chrome.storage.local`, saved profile and resume data is kept in the local Chrome profile for that browser installation.

This extension does not intentionally sync your saved profile or resume across devices.

## Public Source Code

The source code for this extension can be shared publicly without exposing your saved profile details, as long as you do not hardcode your personal information into the project files.

Your saved profile is stored in your own browser's extension storage, not in this repository.

## Removing Your Data

You can remove your saved data by clearing the extension's site data in Chrome or by uninstalling the extension. A built-in clear profile button may be added in a future version.
